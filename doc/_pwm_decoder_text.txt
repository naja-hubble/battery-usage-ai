===== PAGE 1 =====
Battery log file logs battery information below. 
 
1. Event 
 0: Autonomic (either by 30 minute timer or by Battery insertion event) 
 1: Login 
 2: Logoff 
 3: Suspend 
 4: Resume 
 5:AC/DC Power Source Change 
 
2. Charge status 
 0: No activity (no charge or discharge current) 
 1: Charge 
 2: Discharge 
 
3. AC/DC mode 
 0: DC (battery) power 
 1: AC Power 
 
4. Battery Charge remaining (%) 
 
5. Remaining capacity (mWh) 
 
6. Full charge capacity (mWh) 
 
7. Cycle count 
 
8. Remaining Time 
 When Charging: Average Time to Full (estimate of remaining time to full charge) 
 When Discharging: Average Time to Empty (estimate of remaining time to battery 
empty) 
 
9. Total Charged Capacity (Wh) 
10. Total Battery Awake Hrs 
11. Hours at/near Full Charge 
12. Hours at High Temperature (>45 deg C) 
13. Hours at Full Charge and High Temperature
